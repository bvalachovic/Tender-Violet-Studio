About

Tender Violet Studio

I'm the designer behind Tender Violet Studio, a small graphic design studio focused entirely on weddings. I design save-the-dates, invitations, day-of signage, wedding websites, and the small details that tie a wedding day together.

I only take on a handful of weddings at a time, which means I'm not juggling a big roster — I actually take the time to think about you and your story. We'll make sure you feel supported and excited about your special day.

Most of my work comes from word of mouth rather than advertising, so if you found me through a friend or a past client: thank you, and welcome.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me spending time with my own family..
